## New
- **Muted words and hashtags:** in Settings, add words, phrases or #hashtags to hide posts, replies and notifications that mention them. Words match whole words only, so muting "art" won't hide "start". The list is saved to your Nostr mute list, so other apps can use it too
- **Notifications for every account:** each account on your phone is now checked for notifications, not just the one you're using. With more than one account, each notification says which account it's for, and tapping it opens that account
- **Blurred previews:** pictures show a soft, blurred preview while they load, when the post includes one
- **Full-size profile pictures and banners:** tap the picture or banner on a profile to see it full screen, with pinch to zoom
- **Lighter on data:** notifications now catch up by downloading only what's missing, on relays that support it, instead of the last two weeks again every time you open the app

## Fixed
- Posts that couldn't be sent no longer get stuck behind one that's waiting for a relay that's gone
- Saved posts no longer pile up forever: pictures, videos and older posts are cleaned up too, while your own posts and bookmarks are always kept
- Muting someone, a word or a hashtag now hides posts already on screen right away
- Switching feeds while one was still loading could leave connections open in the background, using data and battery
- Loading older posts at the bottom of a feed is faster, and no longer stops working after an error
- Signing out of one account no longer stops notifications for your other accounts
- Each account's notification setting is respected, and turning notifications off for one account no longer affects the others
- Pictures could occasionally be saved incorrectly when two downloads of the same picture happened at once
