## New
- **More than one account:** add another account in Settings and switch between them with a tap. Each account keeps its own messages, wallet and keys, completely separate from the others
- **Posting without a connection:** if you post, react, vote or send a message while offline, Zino keeps it and sends it once you're back online. It shows in the app straight away, and a short message tells you it's waiting
- **Faster message catch-up:** on relays that support it, Zino now compares the messages it already has with the relay and downloads only the ones it's missing, which saves data and time when you open the app
- **Ready for translation:** all of Zino's text can now be translated, and Zino shows up in Android's per-app language setting

## Fixed
- A post that was deleted could sometimes reappear if the deletion arrived before the post
- Warnings sent by relays are now shown in the relay's problems instead of being ignored

## Good to know
- Updating keeps your current account and everything in it
- Notifications are checked for the account you're using
- Signing out removes only the account you're in; your other accounts stay
- Anything that still can't be sent after three days is dropped, and relays that refuse a post outright still show an error
