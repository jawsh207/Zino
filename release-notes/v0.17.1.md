## Fixed
- Zino could freeze and be closed by Android as "not responding" when a playing video scrolled off the screen on a weak connection. Videos now stop and clean up without holding up the app
- Relays that can't compare lists are remembered, so catching up on notifications and messages no longer waits on them
- Relays that are down or have gone away are left alone for longer each time, up to a day, even after Zino restarts, instead of being retried every few minutes. They're used again as soon as they answer
- Paid and read-only relays that turn down your posts aren't sent more for a while
- Zino no longer downloads every follow list from the big general relays once a day, which could get it slowed down by Damus right after starting
