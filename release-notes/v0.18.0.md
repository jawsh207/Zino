## New
- **Shortcuts on the app icon:** press and hold Zino's icon for New post, Messages and Scan QR code. Scanning opens a profile, a post or a wallet connection straight away
- **Home screen widgets:** three to choose from in your launcher's widget list
  - **Write a post:** one tap to a new post
  - **Wallet:** your balance as Zino last saw it, with the time it was checked. Tap the eye to hide the amount, or tap the widget to open your wallet and check again
  - **Following:** the newest posts from people you follow, without anything you've muted. Tap a post to open it, or the pencil to write one. New posts come in while Zino is open and with each background notification check
- Widgets show the account you're using, and switch with it
