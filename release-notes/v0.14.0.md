## New
- **React to messages:** long-press a message in a chat to react with an emoji. Reactions show under the message, and only the people in the chat can see them
- **Reply to messages:** long-press a message and choose Reply. Your reply shows what it's answering, and tapping that takes you back to the original
- **Linked posts in chats:** links to posts and articles in a message now show as preview cards
- **Copy a message:** long-press a message to copy its text

## Good to know
- Reactions and replies work with other Nostr apps that support them, like 0xchat and Amethyst
- The first time you open Zino after updating, it reads your private messages again once, so reactions and replies people sent you before now appear. This is skipped if you sign in with a remote signer
