## New
- **Pictures in a grid:** posts with several pictures now show them all as a grid, instead of one picture and a list of links. In feeds you see up to four, with "+N" for the rest; open the post to see them all. Tap any picture to swipe through every one
- **More pictures recognized:** picture links without a file ending, which some picture hosts use, are now shown as pictures when link previews are on. Links ending in .heic, .heif or .bmp, or with extra details after a #, work too

## Fixed
- Feeds could stop getting new posts after Zino sat in the background, until the app was fully closed and reopened. Zino now checks for a connection whenever you come back to it or pull to refresh
- Names starting with an emoji showed a broken symbol in place of their initials. Notifications, titles, link previews, statuses and poll options no longer cut emoji in half either
- Relays that don't support counting, or that only accept posts, are no longer asked again and again
- Background notification checks no longer show up in the log as "app opened"
